# Ten fuller-build community residents

Original meshes, photographs/textures and rigs: [Microsoft Rocketbox](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), MIT license, Copyright 2020 Microsoft. Full notice in FULLER-ROCKETBOX-LICENSE.txt.

These are adaptations of online base assets, rather than ten ready-made plus-size scans. Zerconda combined authored casual clothing and head assets, reshaped torso/abdomen/hips/thighs/upper arms/neck/cheeks, retained hands/feet and height, and adjusted upper-arm clearance during animation. Body shapes and everyday American casual styling describe fictional characters, not the citizenship or health of real people.
