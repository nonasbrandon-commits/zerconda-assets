# Ten very slim community residents

Original head/clothing meshes, textures and humanoid rigs: [Microsoft Rocketbox](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), MIT license, Copyright 2020 Microsoft. Full notice: SLIM-ROCKETBOX-LICENSE.txt.

Zerconda combined authored casual clothing and heads, adapted waist/chest/hips/arms/thighs/calves/neck proportions, and retained humanoid rigs, natural head/hand/foot proportions and target heights. These are adapted fictional character designs, not ready-made scans or health assessments of real people. Everyday American casual clothing provides the requested setting.
