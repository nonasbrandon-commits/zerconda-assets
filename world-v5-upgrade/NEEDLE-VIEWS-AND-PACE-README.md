# Needle, scenery and movement — v106

Player walk 3.6 m/s and run/mouse run 7.8 m/s; quicker acceleration. Resident walkers 2.15–2.45 m/s, runners 5.10–5.42 m/s, cyclists 6.2 m/s, scooter riders 5.2 m/s, patrol guards 1.9 m/s. Walk/run animation thresholds and cadence match these modes. Character movement uses elapsed time up to 0.15 s independently of the 0.05 s physics cap; collision movement still uses at most 0.12 m substeps. Keyboard and mouse movement produced matching distances at simulated 10 and 30 FPS; feet stayed grounded.

Needle ground lobby rebuilt as a framed glass-and-masonry pavilion with an opaque black roof and permanently open double doors. Compact desk, bench, planter, attendant and lift walls have explicit collision whether entering naturally or through the lobby action. Natural doorway crossing activates/deactivates lobby movement/camera rules. Removed ground-level reception/elevator/skywalk banners, retaining a single exterior name and a small upper lift marker. Ground slab and upper lounge surface have lift cutouts, eliminating the coplanar ground floor beneath the opaque black cabin floor. Roof also leaves the lift travel path open.

Removed asphalt polygon offset that pushed roads behind turf at high viewing angles. Increased camera depth precision and view range. Ocean now extends to distant fogged water and wraps outside both wooded coastal sides. Exterior terrain continues to distant hills, coastal terrain reaches water, and instanced far forest fills the background instead of ending at rectangular tile edges. Distant trees use shared tiered pine geometry, with fewer instances on mobile.

Validation: JS syntax; pace at 10/30 FPS; grounded shoe test; isolated lobby render and zero overlap beneath cabin floor; desk/wall collision, open door paths, natural entry/exit, and fast approach stopping at the desk in the full world. High-view before/after confirms dark asphalt rather than grass. Scenery render checked from both coastal directions. Actual rendering frame rate remains device-dependent.
