# Thread & Coast

A clothing boutique occupies the lot at (188,61), beside the bowling and scooter district. The city map marks CLOTHING. Nearby actions enter the furnished showroom and open a fitting studio. Twenty named starter looks are divided into ten Men and ten Women collection entries, wearable on the current player avatar. This does not add a separate female body/avatar asset.

Players can mix top, bottom and shoe colors, choose pants or shorts and long or short sleeves, and equip a cap/brimmed hat, sunglasses, purse or crossbody bag. Original accessories attach to animated head/hand/spine bones. Clothing colors and exposed sleeve/leg areas adapt the existing textured character; this is a starter wardrobe, not a separate collection of scanned garment meshes. Current idle/walk/run/dance, driving and seated behavior remain supported.

The fitting camera shows the player; phone layout places the wardrobe beneath the preview. Save & wear persists the selection in browser storage; Cancel restores the saved outfit. All items are free for now, with stable catalog IDs for future commerce. There is no purchase backend, currency charge or shared account inventory yet.

Validation: rendered exterior, character accessories and desktop/phone fitting views without JavaScript errors; tested ten entries per collection, trying a shorts outfit, hat/glasses/purse controls, live player changes, save, cancel, persistence and wearing the outfit outside on phone touch controls.
