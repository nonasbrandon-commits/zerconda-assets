Forest style preference

Use the shared createForestGrassMaterial from forest-ground-cover.js for forest ground additions. Preserve the established grass texture and world scale. Layer trees, ferns, shrubs, natural rocks, and ground cover over it. Do not substitute tinted granite for forest grass. Hiking trails use applyRealisticGravel from realistic-gravel.js: gray small-stone gravel matching the supplied reference, world-scaled with subtle bump detail. Preserve stone floors inside caves.
