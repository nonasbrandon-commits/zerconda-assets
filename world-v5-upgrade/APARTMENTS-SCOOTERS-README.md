# Apartments and electric scooters

The four white low-rise residential buildings now each offer 21 individually numbered one-bedroom apartments: seven per floor on three floors, 84 total. A 24 x 23m footprint uses a double-loaded corridor with four bays on each side and one bay reserved for a switchback stair/lift core. Unit layouts fit inside the remaining bays at about 55 square meters of modeled interior floor area. Each includes a separate bedroom, bathroom with a working door, kitchen, dining furniture, sofa and living area. Only the visited floor/unit is rendered. Front signs, a directory and nearby tap/click actions identify availability, visits, floor travel and claiming.

Apartment claims are free for now and saved per building/unit in browser storage, separately from existing house ownership. This is not shared account ownership and no payment is processed.

The brick tower around (135,54) is removed. A generic electric scooter rental shop occupies the corner around (126,69), marked on the map. Eight original unbranded scooters are available free for now. Hold on phones or use movement keys to ride; drag/steer to turn; release to stop. End a ride to leave the scooter at that location. Any parked scooter can be ridden again. Models and positions persist in this browser. Unattended scooters reset to the shop at 04:00 browser-local time, or on the next visit after collection time. Active riders are not teleported by collection. This is a local simulation, not a shared fleet service or an owner NPC collection animation.

Validation: rendered room, corridor and shop without JavaScript or shader errors; verified all floors have distinct unit IDs, bedroom and bathroom walking routes, claims and storage, floor travel and exit; tested nearby actions on a phone viewport; tested scooter riding with touch hold/release, collision protection for buildings/houses/pier, parking and persistence, pickup and automatic overnight collection.
