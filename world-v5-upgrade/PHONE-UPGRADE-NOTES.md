# Counter phone v412

Replaces the disconnected block receiver with a continuous curved handset seated in a rubber cradle. Adds a bevelled wedge shell, twelve tactile numbered keys with fitted atlas labels, clerk-facing LCD, speaker slots, a line indicator, rubber feet and a coiled handset lead above the counter. PBR plastic/rubber/metal materials; static material-batched geometry; no new dynamic lights or network/gameplay behavior.

Visually inspected both customer and clerk viewpoints in actual WebGL. Store glazing, open/closed door routes, counter collision, original three target positions and 24,768 collision samples against the unchanged layout pass. No character model was purchased/imported: both proposed store staff remain external commercial candidates requiring licensed native files.

Source: store-desk-phone.js called by gun-store-counter.js. Build and repack required. Existing game entry pages only; no new public launch-v412.html.
