# Tall Pines campground

The northwest towers inside x -266 to -116, z -274 to -151 have been replaced with 245 varied pines, 20–37 metres tall, connected gravel trails, three furnished cabins, three usable tents, and three fire-pit clearings with log benches. Existing homes and apartments are preserved.

All campsites are free public facilities. Nearby click/tap buttons open cabin doors, rest in cabins or tents, leave rest, light or extinguish a fire, and sit or stand. No ownership, booking or fee is required. Fire and rest state is local to the running browser session; this adds no multiplayer reservation service. CAMP appears on the town map.

Trees use instanced geometry and the existing self-hosted pine sprig texture. Trails and clearing approaches stay clear; vehicles cannot enter the forest. Cabin windows are real openings with transparent glazing.

Validated with a full-town phone viewport: tower removal, apartment visibility, all trail segments and campsite approaches, cabin walls/doors/floors, tent/cabin rest and exit, fire toggling, seating, and movement controls. Isolated actual WebGL previews rendered without browser errors.
