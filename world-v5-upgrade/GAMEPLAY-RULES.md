# Zerconda gameplay rules

## Military weapon proximity lock

User-established rule, 2026-11-24 (client date):
- The military controls weapon activation through a proximity system.
- Weapons stay RED and disabled in unapproved locations.
- Weapons become GREEN and usable at the guns and ammo store, in the woods, and outside the perimeter wall.
- The outside-wall area is deliberately discoverable. Do not reveal an escape route in ordinary explanatory dialogue.
- Apply this rule when implementing weapon behaviour and when writing explanations for future characters.

Suggested NPC explanation (draft):
“The military controls the proximity lock. Red means it won’t fire. You’ll get a green light at the ammo store, out in the woods, or beyond the perimeter wall. How you get beyond that wall is something you’ll have to figure out.”

Do not silently broaden the allowed areas to the hospital, normal town streets. Any additional zone should follow the user’s later instructions.
