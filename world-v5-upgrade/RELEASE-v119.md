# Z Store and player menu

Added the walk-in Z Store between Scooter Rentals and Thread & Coast, with a staffed counter, wood shelves, glass cases and 42 colored product displays. Flashlights, utility knives, balloons, bouquets, sunglasses, watches and rings can be ordered in six colors and equipped. Orders persist locally in a bag; flashlights switch on/off, knives fold/unfold, watches show local time, and accessories attach to the animated avatar. Future stock can be added through STORE_ITEMS and the model builder.

Replaced the Map/Reset/Beach toolbar with a Player dropdown beside area travel. Profile saves a local display name and opens the bag. Settings controls map visibility, movement help, brightness and graphics quality. Added Z Store to area travel and the uncluttered nearby map.

Validation: module syntax checks; actual browser tests for all seven orders, saved bag/profile reload, wearable attachment, flashlight use, watch, floor/counter/entrance collision, desktop/mobile menus; production-world import, fast travel and movement lock. No optional scenery is added to the startup loading gate.
