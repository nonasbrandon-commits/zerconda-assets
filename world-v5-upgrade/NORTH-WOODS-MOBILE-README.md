# North Woods and mobile update v98

Connected three town streets to a closed airport perimeter road. Moved the northern wall to z=-1000, expanded the ground and landscape, and added dense instanced forest, broad gravel paths, narrow woodland trails, and rest benches.

Mobile: shadows off, lower pixel ratio, smaller textures, merged static building geometry, distance culling, serialized nearby resident model loading (up to six), and 30 fps cap. Removed hold indicator and disabled iOS long-press callouts. Run/Walk button and hold instructions show the active mode.

Validation: Chromium touch emulation confirmed Run/Walk and hold movement, no visible hold indicator, no page errors, and unobstructed road/trail samples. Mobile forest contains 1100 tall trees and 613 shrubs. Actual iPhone Safari stability requires device verification.
