# Seaside pier

A large timber pier with two free-use service shacks on the right when looking out to sea, seating, fishing rails and a lower floating dock. All watercraft designs and names are generic.

Use Beach and walk down the central beach approach. Nearby actions offer free watercraft, fishing gear and seating. Both moored watercraft can be ridden. On phones, hold the world view to move, drag to turn, and release to stop. Keyboard movement and steering are also supported. Return the craft using the nearby action; marked buoys define the safe boating area.

Collect a free rod, choose a marked fishing rail, cast, and tap Reel when a fish bites. Catches are recorded and released. The catch log is stored in this browser, without payment or a multiplayer inventory service.

The ocean uses animated world-space swells, irregular ripple normals, depth-based color, sky and sun reflection, shoreline foam and boat wakes. Watercraft follow the same wave heights.

Validation: rendered ocean and pier without shader errors; checked connected walking routes, rail and building boundaries, both craft, collision boundaries, docking, casting through catch recording, seating, and phone hold/release controls in the full town.
