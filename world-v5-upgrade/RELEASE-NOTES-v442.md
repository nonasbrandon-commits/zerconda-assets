# Zerconda v442

Removed the Beach Cottage Rear Alley, its dumpster, backing wall and collection stop. Restored the beach cottage area; removed its map/travel label. Restored the Town Plaza and West Extension open areas by removing their standalone service roads and backing walls.

Remaining pickups use actual buildings: Services / Z Tattoo, Cityview Apartments on the same through alley, and Luxe Lanes on a separate through alley. Both alleys join public roads with flush sidewalk openings. Services entrance paving follows its existing building edges. Removed the remaining Style Cuts gravel path through to its road endpoint, retaining the grove trees.

Dispatch one sanitation truck at a time. Waiting trucks are hidden and excluded from collisions; a replacement is dispatched only when the previous truck completes its full return loop. Each stop retains a minimum ten-minute interval after collection. This prevents several sanitation trucks bunching up on the public road.

Validation: component collection, lid, boarding, departure, cadence and mobile unload checks; actual packed world boot and alley clearance; packed/privacy audit. Native iPhone Safari remains unverified. No new media assets or dynamic lights.
