# Needle lounge and modern pickup — v90

The Needle has a staffed, street-level reception lobby. Ask the attendant for elevator access, then approach the glass lift. Its 18-second ascent and descent preserve camera look controls and show the actual town during travel.

The observation floor has nine indoor sofa seats and sixteen outdoor bench seats. Four open portals connect the glazed lounge to a continuous outdoor skywalk. Clear glazing, slim framing, a roof above head level, and low transparent guards replace the former overlapping dome geometry. The avatar fades only when the camera is too close to its head.

The original, unbranded Zerconda Horizon Pickup joins the dealership display and two driveable street parking spaces. It uses the existing vehicle entry, driving, and exit controls.

Validation: JavaScript syntax checks; isolated real WebGL previews of the lounge, reception, and pickup; phone-sized touch interaction through reception, both lift directions, sitting/standing, and pickup entry/exit. Geometry checks confirm all four portal routes, a continuous outer walking loop, indoor and outdoor sightlines, floor support, and safe perimeter bounds. The ride midpoint reaches about 45 metres. No browser page errors in the interaction test.

Seats are available gathering locations within the existing world. This change does not add a multiplayer backend.
