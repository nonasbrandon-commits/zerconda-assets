# Zerconda Luxe Lanes

An original art-deco bowling venue occupies the land behind the scooter shop at (140,35), with west-facing entrance at (117.6,35). The footprint, entrance path, and neighboring tree/tower clearance stay behind the road sidewalks. Exterior gold trim, glass, lit accents, canopy and bowling sculptures distinguish the venue. The city map marks BOWLING.

Enter using the standard nearby tap/click action. The interior has six full-length lanes, lounge seats, tables and a counter. Approach a lane and choose Play bowling. Aim, release position, power and curve sliders control the throw. Roll ball starts an animated ball and pin collision simulation. Gutters miss the rack, pins propagate collision impulses, remaining pins carry over to the second throw, and full racks reset after a strike/frame. Lane camera follows the action. Leave game restores walking; Exit bowling alley returns outside. Mouse, keyboard F nearby actions and phone touch controls are supported.

Ten-frame scoring includes strike/spare bonuses and tenth-frame extra rolls. Best five completed scores save in this browser. This is a single-player local minigame; it does not add shared lane booking, multiplayer turns or payment.

Validation: rendered exterior, interior and game without JavaScript or shader errors; tested phone entry, two rolls, lounge sitting/standing and exit in the full town; checked blocked playing surfaces and exterior walls; checked gutter outcome, a complete 12-strike 300 game and score persistence; verified pure scoring for 300, 150 spares, zero and open-frame games and tenth-frame bonus cases.
